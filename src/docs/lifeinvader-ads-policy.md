## 1. General Rules & Ad Format

LifeInvader ads always follow a fixed **action → detail → price/budget** structure. Getting this skeleton right is the single most important skill for an editor, because every category (real estate, auto, work, etc.) is just a variation on this same backbone.

### 1.1 The Four Core Verbs

| Intent             | Verb to use            | Price field        | Example                                                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Selling only       | **Selling**            | Price              | Selling pants for men. Price: Negotiable.                   |
| Selling or trading | **Selling or trading** | Price              | Selling or trading "Ubermacht M5 (E34)". Price: Negotiable. |
| Trading only       | **Trading**            | _(no price field)_ | Trading "Ubermacht M5 (E34)".                               |
| Buying             | **Buying**             | Budget             | Buying seeds. Budget: Negotiable.                           |

- "Looking to buy" is always normalized to **Buying**.
  - Raw: _Looking to buy a house_ → Edited: **Buying a house. Budget: Negotiable.**
- If the customer doesn't state a price or budget, you must still add the field:
  - **Price: Negotiable.** or **Budget: Negotiable.**
- A colon (`:`) always follows the word Price or Budget — never a dash, equals sign, or nothing.

### 1.2 The Cross-Category Trading Rule

> **You cannot edit ads that trade items from two different categories.** A car cannot be traded for a house or a business, and vice versa. If a raw ad tries to do this, it must be rejected rather than "fixed" — this is not an editable formatting issue, it's an invalid ad.

### 1.3 Money Formatting Rules

These are some of the most commonly-missed rules, so read carefully:

1. **Dollar sign first.** Every numerical value is preceded by `$`.
2. **No trailing period after a number.** If the ad ends in a numeric value, do not add a full stop.
   - Correct: `Price: $4.000`
   - Correct: `Price: $4 Million.` _(ends in the word "Million", so the period stays)_
3. **Never abbreviate with K or M.** Spell out thousands in full, and use the word "Million" for millions.

   | Raw   | Corrected     |
   | ----- | ------------- |
   | $1k   | $1.000        |
   | $10k  | $10.000       |
   | $100k | $100.000      |
   | $1m   | $1 Million.   |
   | $14m  | $14 Million.  |
   | $4.5m | $4.5 Million. |

4. **Use a period, not a comma, as the thousands separator.**
   - Correct: `$1.000`
   - Incorrect: `$1,000`
5. **Billion-plus values become "Negotiable"** — with two exceptions: **houses and businesses**, which are allowed to display amounts over $1 Billion.
   - Raw: _Selling a car. Price 2 Billion._ → Edited: **Selling a car. Price: Negotiable.**
6. **Minimum thresholds convert to Negotiable:**
   - A house listed under **$1 Million** → change price to Negotiable.
   - An apartment listed under **$2 Million** → change price to Negotiable.

### 1.4 General Editing Notes

- Always cross-check clothing items and vehicles/bikes/planes/helicopters against the **clothing list** and **vehicle list** documents for exact naming.
- Brand names and location names must be **capitalised** (see the capitalisation list in Section 3, Real Estate).
- When a specific quantity of items is mentioned, the number takes a period as its thousands separator, same as money.
  - Example: **Selling 1.000 seeds. Price: Negotiable.**
- **Always double-check** the ad is correct before posting.
- **Always select the correct category** for the ad.
- Trading two items from different categories does **not** get filed under "Other" — it gets rejected outright (see 1.2 above).
- You are entitled to **reject** any ad that is improper, incomprehensible, or offensive in any way.
- Some rejected ads must also be **blacklisted** — see Section 2 below for the specific list and durations.

## 3. Real Estate

### 3.1 General Rules

- **Max 2 properties per ad.** Houses are **not** tradeable.
  - Example: **Selling houses №213 and №767. Price: Negotiable.**
- If a property number is given, use the **№** symbol.
  - Example: **Selling house №758. Price: Negotiable.**
- **Garage spaces** can only be **2, 5, 9, 25, or 30**, abbreviated as **g.s.**
  - Example: **Selling a house with 5 g.s. Price: Negotiable.**
- **Warehouse spaces** can only be **3, 4, or 5**, abbreviated as **w.h.**
  - Example: **Selling a house with 4 w.h. Price: Negotiable.**
- If a property has insurance, just say **with insurance** — no need to give a day count.
- Extras that can be included: garden, insurance, view, tennis court, swimming pool, helipad, long driveway, etc.
  - Example: **Selling a house with a garden, helipad and swimming pool. Price: Negotiable.**

### 3.2 Mandatory Feature Order

When multiple features are listed, they must always appear in this exact sequence:

1. Family (advanced, elite)
2. Garden
3. Garage spaces (2/5/9/25/30 g.s.)
4. Warehouse spaces (3/4/5 w.h.)
5. Custom interior
6. Insurance
7. Others (helipad, tennis court, long driveway, swimming pool)
8. View (nice, beautiful, great, good view)
9. Location (e.g. in Vinewood Hills, near post office)

**Full example:**

> Selling house №758 with a garden, 30 g.s., 5 w.h., custom interior, insurance, helipad and great view in Vinewood Hills. Price: Negotiable.

> Selling an apartment with 9 g.s., custom interior, insurance and a great view in the Casino. Price: Negotiable.

### 3.3 Custom Interior Rule

- "Custom interior" is allowed if the sender says the property is furnished / has different interiors.
- **CAREFUL:** You may **never** mention nationality-themed interiors (Italian interior, Russian interior, Chinese interior, etc.) — strip these out entirely and replace with "custom interior."

### 3.4 Plural Rules for Multiple Properties

If two houses are mentioned, pluralize:

- garden → gardens
- helipad → helipads
- tennis court → tennis courts
- long driveway → long driveways
- swimming pool → swimming pools

"Insurance" and "custom interior" stay singular (no "s") even with two properties.

Examples:

> Selling 2 houses with gardens, helipads, tennis courts, long driveways and swimming pools. Price: Negotiable.

> Selling houses №766 and №767 with gardens, 25 g.s., insurance and swimming pools. Price: Negotiable.

Apartments follow the same singular rule for insurance/custom interior:

> Selling 2 apartments with 9 g.s., 5 w.h. and custom interior. Price: Negotiable.

> Selling apartments №766 and №767 with 25 g.s., 5 w.h. and custom interior. Price: Negotiable.

### 3.5 Mixed House + Apartment Ads

If the customer mentions one house and one apartment:

> Selling a house and an apartment. Price: Negotiable.
> Buying a house and an apartment. Budget: Negotiable.
> Buying a house or an apartment. Budget: Negotiable.

Since apartments don't have gardens, only mention selling/buying the house and apartment as a pair — no extras.

**You cannot buy a specific house or apartment.** If a "buying" ad mentions a property number, strip it to "Buying a house" / "Buying an apartment" — or reject with: _"We can NOT buy specific houses/apartments."_

### 3.6 Renting

- Instead of Selling // Price → use **Renting out // Rent**.
- Instead of Buying // Budget → use **Looking to rent // Budget**.

> Renting out house №758. Rent: Negotiable.
> Looking to rent a house. Budget: Negotiable.

**CAREFUL:** "Renting out" = you are giving up your property to someone else. "Looking to rent" = you want to rent a property from someone else. Don't mix these up.

Rent duration can be per day or per week:

> Renting out a house. Rent: $200.000 for 7 days.
> Renting out a house. Rent: $200.000 per week.

### 3.7 Miscellaneous Real Estate Rules

- Always put **two periods** on abbreviations `g.s.` and `w.h.`, even before a comma: _"with a garden, 9 g.s., 5 w.h. and swimming pool."_
- **Never** mention the green zone or any gang location/name.
- Use **mansion** instead of villa.
  - Selling a mansion. Price: Negotiable.
  - Selling mansion №25. Price: Negotiable.
- Use **Casino penthouse** instead of Casino apartment.
  - Selling a Casino penthouse. Price: Negotiable.
  - Selling Casino penthouse №123. Price: Negotiable.

### 3.8 Families

**General rules:**

- Family names must **never** be mentioned in any ad.
- Only **one family per ad**.
- Prices must be close to market value; unrealistic/troll pricing results in the price being changed to Negotiable, or the ad being rejected and potentially blacklisted.
- Families **cannot** be sold with a specific property unless it's clearly a combined deal (house/apartment + family).

**Tier pricing reference:**

| Tier    | Type            | Price       |
| ------- | --------------- | ----------- |
| Level 3 | Advanced Family | $10,000,000 |
| Level 4 | Elite Family    | $10,000,000 |

**Examples by tier:**

- General (no tier): _Selling a family. Price: Negotiable._ / _Buying a family. Budget: Negotiable._
- Level 3 — Advanced: _Selling an advanced family. Price: $12 Million._ / _Buying an advanced family. Budget: $10 Million._
- Level 4 — Elite: _Selling an elite family. Price: $18 Million._ / _Buying an elite family. Budget: Negotiable._

**Combined property + family examples:**

- Selling a house with a family. Price: Negotiable.
- Selling house №3 with an elite family. Price: $50 Million. _(combined price)_
- Selling a house with an elite family, garden, 25 g.s. and helipad. Price: $100 Million.
- Selling apartment №1552 with a family. Price: $17 Million.

### 3.9 Location Capitalisation

**Uppercase / capitalised locations** (use "at," "near," "in" as shown):
Amphitheater №1/2 (party ads only), Auto Salon, Bahama Mamas Bar, Banham Canyon, Business Center, Capitol, the Casino, Cayo Perico Island, Chumash, the Church, Del Perro, Diamond Bar, Downtown Vinewood, Eclipse Tower, El Burro Heights, Fight Club, Hospital/Sandy Hospital, Legion Square (party ads only), LifeInvader, Little Seoul, Mirror Park, Richards Majestic, Richman, Rockford Hills, Pacific Bluffs Country Club, Paleto Bay, Pillbox Hill, Rancho, Sandy Shores, Tequi-La-La Bar, Vanilla Unicorn Bar, Vespucci Canals, Vinewood Hills, the Yacht (party ads only), West Vinewood.

**Lowercase locations:**
airport (business ads only), beach (party ads only), beach market, city (house & business ads only), fire station, ghetto, mall (near/in, business ads only), post office, Residential Complex №1–№8, stadium, train station.

## 4. Auto

### 4.1 General Rules

- **Max 1 vehicle per ad**, unless trading.
  - Selling: **Selling "Ubermacht M3 (G80)". Price: Negotiable.**
  - Trade: **Selling or trading "Ubermacht M3 (G80)" for "Grotti Italia (F458)". Price: Negotiable.**
- This category covers **cars, motorcycles, planes, helicopters, and boats**.
- Brand + model always go in **quotation marks** ("").
  - Selling "Ubermacht M3 (G80)". Price: Negotiable.
- Always cross-reference the **sellable car/motorcycle/boat/helicopter/plane list** for correct naming.

### 4.2 Vehicle Feature Terminology

| Sender says...                                       | Editor writes...             |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Max config / max chip tuning / fully upgraded        | **in full configuration**    |
| Nearly max / level 3 or below                        | **in partial configuration** |
| Body upgrades / body kit                             | **visual upgrades**          |
| Engine/suspension/tyres/brakes/transmission upgrades | **tuning parts**             |
| Turbo or drift box                                   | **turbo and drift kit**      |

- Use **"in configuration,"** never "with configuration."
- If insured, just add **"with insurance"** — no day count needed.

### 4.3 Mandatory Feature Order (Auto)

1. Configuration (partial or full)
2. Visual upgrades
3. Insurance
4. Tuning parts
5. Turbo kit
6. Drift kit

**Full examples:**

> Selling a car in full configuration. Price: Negotiable.
> Selling a car in full configuration with visual upgrades. Price: Negotiable.
> Selling a car in full configuration with visual upgrades and insurance. Price: Negotiable.
> Selling a car in full configuration with visual upgrades, insurance, turbo and drift kit. Price: Negotiable.
> Selling a car in full configuration with visual upgrades, insurance, tuning parts, turbo and drift kit. Price: Negotiable.
> Buying a car in partial configuration with turbo kit. Budget: Negotiable.

### 4.4 Character Limit Overflow (Last Resort Only)

If the full ad exceeds the 150-character limit and throws an error, simplify by dropping the brand/model quote details or shortening feature lists — but only as a last resort:

> Selling "Ocelot Vanquish Zagato SB" in partial configuration, visual upgrades, insurance, tuning parts, turbo and drift kit. Price: Negotiable.

### 4.5 Electric Cars & Trading

- Electric car ads are allowed: **Buying an electric car. Budget: Negotiable.**
- If a sender gives no brand, write generically: _"Selling a car/motorbike/bike/motorcycle/plane/helicopter/boat."_
- If citizens are **only trading**, do **not** mention Budget/Price at all:
  > Trading "Ubermacht M5 (E34)" for "Ubermacht X7 (G07)".
- If a vehicle isn't found in the list, **reject the ad**. If the sender provides proof of tradeability, escalate to LI/HC to have the list updated.

## 5. Dating

### 5.1 Allowed Ad Types

Only these categories are permitted:

- Looking for a specific person
- Looking for a family
- Looking for family members
- Looking for family diplomacy _(replaces "family friends/alliances")_
- Looking for friends / a friend
- Looking for a wife / husband
- Looking for a girlfriend / boyfriend
- Looking for a date

### 5.2 Looking for a Specific Person — Process

1. **Require full name** (first + last). If missing, reject with: _"Please provide full name."_
2. Search the name in the **Grand RP Discord database** (press ENTER if not appearing) or the **LI database** (Backup City Database channel).
3. Capitalise the first letter of both first and last name (e.g. _Olivia Johnson_).
4. Check the person's roles — **you cannot look for:**
   - Admins/gods (EN1 Administrators). Exception: if they hold admin roles on a _different_ server but have the normal "EN | Player" role here, you may look for them. Same logic applies to Project Management, PR Managers, and the Community Manager.
   - Leaders/Deputies of a state organisation.
5. If not found in the databases, reject with: _"Person not found in the database. Please contact LI to provide proof of existence."_
6. If the client provides proof of existence, post the proof photo in the **Backup City Database** channel with the name attached, for future searches.
7. If you personally know the person exists but they aren't in the database yet, you may accept the ad and log them into the Backup City Database for others.
8. **Only 1 person per ad.** Reject multi-person searches with: _"Cannot look for more than 1 person at a time."_

### 5.3 Looking for a Family / Family Members

- **Never** mention a family's specific nationality.
- Looking for a **specific** family is **not allowed**.
- Family recruitment ads are **not allowed** — reject with: _"Family recruitment ads are not allowed. Please contact LI to request a service."_

### 5.4 Other Dating Rules

- Looking for an Administration Assistant → **allowed**.
- Looking for a Leader of an _unofficial_ org → **allowed**.
- Looking for a leader/deputy leader of a **Crime** organisation (EN | Crime role) → **allowed**.

### 5.5 Blacklisted "Looking For" Ads

- Buying a wife/husband.
- Troll ads (e.g. "looking for a sugar daddy/mommy").
- Looking for a wife/husband with a price/budget attached.
- Any troll name not found in the database.
- Any troll name that's offensive in a foreign language, even if found in the database — **inform HC immediately** if spotted.
- People looking for themselves.
- Looking for lesbian/gay individuals.
- Looking for sex/a hookup.

## 6. Work

### 6.1 Core Verbs

- **Hiring** (employer looking for workers)
- **Looking for a job** (worker looking for employment)
- Always use **Salary** in place of Price/Budget, even if unmentioned:
  > Looking for a job. Salary: Negotiable.

### 6.2 Construction Sites

If a construction site number is given, include location + № symbol:

> Hiring workers at construction site №1, on Vespucci Boulevard. Salary: Negotiable.
> Hiring workers at construction site №2, on Calais Avenue. Salary: Negotiable.
> Hiring workers at construction site №3, in Pillbox Hill. Salary: Negotiable.
> Hiring workers at construction site №4, in Mirror Park. Salary: Negotiable.

- Never use the word **"level."** Use **"years of experience"** instead.
  > Hiring a driver with 3 years of experience at construction site №2, on Calais Avenue. Salary: Negotiable.

### 6.3 Construction Site Roles (only 5 allowed)

1. **Locksmith** (replaces "lumberjack")
2. **Electrician**
3. **Land worker** (replaces "farmer")
4. **Surveyor** (replaces "oilman")
5. **Driver**

Only one role may be advertised per ad.

### 6.4 Other Permitted Jobs

1. Trucker
2. Lawyer
3. DJ
4. Photographer/cameraman
5. Bodyguard
6. Professional dancer (**never** "stripper"/"pole dancer")
7. Oil man
8. Gardener
9. Pilot
10. Collector
11. Firefighter
12. Personal assistant
13. Singer

Rate can be specified as **per hour** or **per day**:

> Hiring a DJ. Salary: $100.000 per hour.
> Hiring a bodyguard. Salary: $100.000 per day.

**Only ONE job per ad.** If more than one is mentioned, reject with: _"Only one work allowed per AD."_

### 6.5 Work Examples

- Hiring workers at construction site. Salary: Negotiable.
- Looking for work as a trucker with 3 years of experience. Salary: Negotiable.
- Hiring a driver with 3 years of experience. Salary: $10.000 per hour.
- Hiring a gardener. Salary: $40.000
- Hiring professional dancers. Salary: Negotiable.
- Hiring workers for solar panel plantation. Salary: Negotiable.
- Hiring workers for collector job. Salary: Negotiable.
- Hiring a personal assistant. Salary: Negotiable.
- Hiring a lawyer. Salary: Negotiable.

> **Note:** All office, oil well, and farm templates go under the **Work** category, even though they might sound like "Business."

## 7. Businesses

### 7.1 General Rules

- **Only one business per ad.** More than one → reject with: _"Only one business allowed per AD"_ and note **trading of businesses is not allowed** — only selling and buying.
- Use **"private business"** instead of "personal."
  > Selling a private business. Price: Negotiable.
- A drug lab business is always referred to as a **"Burger Shop"** — nothing else.
- **"Family business"** is an allowed term:
  > Buying a family business. Budget: $50 Million.
- If a business number is given, **drop the word "business":**
  > Selling 24/7 Store №27. Price: $60 Million.

- If a location is given, include it:
  > Buying Electric Charging Station business in Sandy Shores. Budget: $60 Million.
- If a client just says "Buying a business," normalize it to **"Buying a private business."**

### 7.2 Approved Business Categories (correct capitalisation)

### 7.3 Plantation Sub-Rules

**Only 4 crop types exist:**

## 10. Other

### 10.1 General Rules

- **Max 3 items** per ad in this category.
- Covers: Clothing/accessories/shoes, bags/backpacks, resources, seeds, fish, containers, pets, tickets, and general items.

### 10.2 Clothing Ad Word Order

1. Colour
2. Luminous
3. Brand
4. Type of clothing (pants, T-shirt, etc.)
5. "of type"
6. For men/for women

Optional adjectives: professional, luxury, stylish, casual, cheap, affordable.

**Substitution rules:**

- "Men's / women's" → **for men / for women**
- "Extra" → **of type**
- "Rare / legendary" → **exclusive / unique**

### 10.3 Quantities

- If a specific quantity of seeds/fruit/etc. is given, there's no need to also say "in bulk."
- Quantities use the period thousands-separator, same as money (e.g. **1.000 seeds**).

### 10.4 Beach Market

- **Never** mention prices in beach market shop ads.
- "Cheap prices" → rewrite as **"for good prices."**

**Examples:**

> Selling a variety of items for good prices at beach market shop №27.
> Selling juices for good prices at beach market shop №21.
> Selling desert scarf mask for good price at beach market shop №23.

### 10.5 License Plates

- Max **1 license plate per ad.**
- If the plate contains bad/negative/provocative characters, use the generic "special license plate" format instead of showing the actual plate.

**Examples:**

> Selling a special license plate. Price: Negotiable.
> Selling license plate (1ABC234). Price: Negotiable.
> Buying a special license plate. Budget: Negotiable.

### 10.6 Sim Cards

- Max **2 sim-cards per ad.**
- Format must be **XX-XX-XXX**.

## 12. Grammar & Format Guide

### 12.1 "A" vs "An"

**Rule of thumb:** it's about _sound_, not spelling.

- **"A"** goes before a noun with a **consonant sound** — B, C, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, X, Y, Z.
  - Examples: battery, campfire, diamond, flag, grand ticket, hookah, juice, kerosene barrels, luminous stone, map of Los Santos, new year gift, pet, repair kit, solar panel, thread, video card, wire.
- **"An"** goes before a noun with a **vowel sound** — A, E, I, O, U.
  - Examples: animal skin, elixir, ingrand container, oil barrel.

**Base examples:**

> Selling a repair kit. Price: Negotiable.
> Buying an animal skin. Budget: Negotiable.
> Trading a video card for an oil barrel.

### 12.2 Exceptions

**"H" words** — depends on whether the "h" sound is pronounced:

- Pronounced "h" (hat, hotel) → use **"a."**
  > Selling a hookah. Price: Negotiable.
- Silent "h" (honor, hour, honest) → use **"an."**
  > Selling an hourglass. Price: Negotiable. _(illustrative — item may not exist in-game)_

**"U" words** — depends on whether it sounds like "you" or "uh":

- Sounds like "you" (user, usual, utilized) → use **"a."**
  > Selling a unique love container. Price: Negotiable.
- Sounds like "uh" (unusual, understanding, utter) → use **"an."**
  > Selling an umbrella. Price: Negotiable. _(illustrative — item may not exist in-game)_

### 12.3 Singular & Plural Nouns

**Standard rule** — add **-s**:

> house → houses, pet → pets, snowball → snowballs

**Nouns ending in s, x, z, ch, sh** — add **-es**:

> bus → buses, box → boxes, leash → leashes

**Consonant + y** — drop the y, add **-ies**:

> ruby → rubies, battery → batteries, city → cities

### 12.4 Irregular Nouns

**Irregular plurals:**

> elf → elves, woman → women, man → men, mouse → mice, wife → wives, scarf → scarves

**Same form singular/plural** (never add "s"):

> carp, copper, dice, fish, fuel (for resource extraction), milk, salmon, metal, timber

**Always-plural-only nouns:**

> fireworks, binoculars, brakes, earplugs

### 12.5 Compound Item Names — Pluralisation

**Critical rule:** when an item name is made of 2+ words, only the **second word** becomes plural — not the first.

| Correct plural                | Why                                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Selling 2 pets.               | single-word item pluralizes normally        |
| Selling 2 pet rats.           | compound — only "rat" pluralizes, not "pet" |
| Selling 30 mushrooms.         | single word                                 |
| Selling 30 mushroom seeds.    | compound — only "seed" pluralizes           |
| Selling 2 scarves.            | single word, irregular plural               |
| Selling 2 desert scarf masks. | compound — only "mask" pluralizes           |

### 12.6 Disclaimer

This grammar guide covers _general_ rules. There will be edge cases where the rules above don't cleanly apply — when in doubt, **ask a Head of Category (HC)** how to properly edit that specific ad rather than guessing.

## 13. Master Step-by-Step: How to Edit an Ad on LifeInvader

This is the full walkthrough an editor should mentally run through, top to bottom, every single time a raw ad comes in — regardless of category.

### Step 1 — Read the Raw Ad Fully First

Do not start editing mid-read. Read the entire raw submission once, in full, so you understand:

- What the sender actually wants (sell, buy, trade, hire, rent, look for someone).
- How many items/properties/vehicles/people are involved.
- Whether anything in it looks illegal, offensive, or troll-like.

### Step 2 — Identify the Category

Decide which of the 9 categories the ad belongs to:
**Real Estate · Auto · Dating · Work · Businesses · Service · Discounts · Other · (or a straight rejection).**

If the ad mixes two categories (e.g. a car AND a house), this is an automatic **rejection** — do not attempt to split or merge it (Section 1.2).

### Step 3 — Run the Illegal/Blacklist Check First

Before any formatting work, scan the raw text against the **Section 2 lists**:

- Is this a rejected-and-blacklisted item (weapons, drugs, ORG items, scanners, etc.)? → Reject **and** blacklist for the listed duration, then stop.
- Is this a rejected-only item (family names, nationality, forbidden locations, gambling, etc.)? → Reject, no blacklist, then stop.
- If clean, proceed to Step 4.

### Step 4 — Check Quantity Limits

Depending on category, confirm the sender hasn't exceeded:

- 2 properties (Real Estate)
- 1 vehicle (Auto, unless trading)
- 1 business (Businesses)
- 3 items (Other)
- 1 job (Work)
- 1 person (Dating)
- 1 license plate / 2 sim-cards (Other)

If exceeded, reject with the matching standard reason (Section 2.5) — e.g. _"Only one business allowed per AD."_

### Step 5 — Normalize the Core Verb

Convert whatever phrasing the sender used into the correct standard verb:

- "Looking to buy" → **Buying**
- "Want to sell/get rid of" → **Selling**
- "Want to swap/exchange" → **Trading**
- "Need workers/staff" → **Hiring**
- "Want a job as..." → **Looking for a job**
- "Want to rent my place out" → **Renting out**
- "Want a place to rent" → **Looking to rent**

### Step 6 — Apply Category-Specific Terminology Substitutions

Run the raw text through the substitution table for that category:

- **Real estate:** villa → mansion, Casino apartment → Casino penthouse, garage/warehouse counts → g.s./w.h.
- **Auto:** max config → full configuration, body kit → visual upgrades, engine/suspension upgrades → tuning parts, turbo/drift box → turbo and drift kit.
- **Work:** lumberjack → locksmith, farmer → land worker, oilman → surveyor, "level X" → "X years of experience," stripper → professional dancer.
- **Businesses:** personal business → private business, drug lab → Burger Shop, gun/weapon store → Ammunition Store, Binco/Suburban → Clothing Shop.
- **Other:** rare/legendary → exclusive/unique, men's/women's → for men/for women, extra → of type.

### Step 7 — Reorder Features Into Mandatory Sequence

If the category has a fixed feature order (Real Estate or Auto), rearrange whatever order the sender wrote them in into the official sequence:

- **Real Estate order:** family → garden → garage spaces → warehouse spaces → custom interior → insurance → others → view → location.
- **Auto order:** configuration → visual upgrades → insurance → tuning parts → turbo kit → drift kit.

### Step 8 — Apply Grammar Rules

- Choose **a/an** correctly based on sound, not spelling (Section 12.1–12.2).
- Pluralize correctly, remembering the **compound-word rule**: only the second word of a two-word item pluralizes (Section 12.5).
- Capitalise brand names and any location from the approved capitalisation list (Section 3.9).
- Put vehicle brand/model names in **quotation marks**.

### Step 9 — Format the Price/Budget/Salary/Rent/Bet Field

- Choose the correct field name for the category: **Price** (selling), **Budget** (buying), **Salary** (work), **Rent** (renting), **Bet** (games).
- Add a colon after the field name.
- Add `$` before any number.
- Convert K/M shorthand into full numbers or "X Million."
- Use periods, not commas, as separators.
- Apply the Negotiable override rules for amounts under/over the relevant threshold (Section 1.3).
- If nothing was stated, default to **Negotiable**.

### Step 10 — Cross-Reference the Databases

- For **vehicles**: check the sellable car/motorcycle/boat/plane/helicopter list.
- For **clothing/items**: check the clothing list document.
- For **specific people** (Dating): check the Grand RP Discord and the LI "Backup City Database" channel.
- If not found anywhere, reject using the matching standard rejection reason and ask the sender for proof.

### Step 11 — Final Read-Through

Before posting, re-read the fully edited ad one more time and confirm:

- The category is correct.
- The verb, price field, and formatting are all correct.
- The feature order (if applicable) is correct.
- No illegal items, blacklisted terms, or excess quantities slipped through.
- Grammar (a/an, plurals) is correct.
- The character limit is respected (fall back to the shortened last-resort format only if truly necessary — Auto category).

### Step 12 — Post or Reject

- If everything checks out, **post the ad** in its final, corrected format.
- If anything fails at any step above, **reject** with the short, professional, standard rejection reason — and apply a blacklist duration if the item/category calls for one (Section 2.1).

### Step 13 — When In Doubt

If a raw ad presents an edge case not clearly covered by any rule above (unusual grammar, an item not on any list, an ambiguous category), **do not guess** — ask a **Head of Category (HC)** before posting or rejecting.
