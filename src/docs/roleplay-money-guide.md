# 💰 Money-Making Guide — How to Earn on the Server

This guide covers **every legal and illegal way** to make money in-game, organized into **income tiers** so you know what to expect at each stage of your character's journey. 🎮

⚠️ **Reminder:** Illegal activities still follow the [Fair Play Rules](./roleplay-fairplay-rules) — no PowerGaming, no UB, and always roleplay realistically. Getting caught is part of the risk!

---

## 🟢 TIER 1 — Starter Income (₹0 → ₹100K per day)

This is where every new character starts. Slow, steady, low-risk money.

### 👔 Legal Jobs (Office/Basic Jobs)

- 🚕 Taxi/Uber Driver — pick up NPC or player fares
- 🚛 Trucker — delivery runs between depots
- 🌾 Farmer — harvesting crops/collecting seeds, selling to shops
- 🎣 Fisherman — catching and selling fish
- 🪵 Lumberjack — cutting/selling wood
- ⛏️ Miner — mining ores/materials for basic resale
- 🍔 Fast Food / Restaurant Worker — cooking, serving
- 🧹 Cleaner / Janitor Jobs — basic city maintenance work
- 📦 Mail Courier — delivering packages around the city
- 🅿️ Valet / Parking Attendant

### 🏢 Entry-Level Office Jobs

- 📞 Call Center / Receptionist roles
- 🧾 Cashier at a store
- 🛠️ Mechanic Apprentice (basic repairs before upgrading)

### 🎯 Small Side Hustles

- 🎮 Daily login bonuses / rewards
- 🎫 Small in-game quests or NPC tasks
- 🛍️ Selling starter crafted items (basic crafting)
- 🅿️ Recycling / scrap collecting

> 💡 **Beginner tip:** Stack a legal job + small side hustles. This tier is about survival money — rent, food, and basic gear.

---

## 🟡 TIER 2 — Growth Income (₹100K → ₹1 Million per week)

Once you're established, you can specialize and start earning steadier weekly income.

### 👔 Mid Level Skilled Jobs

- 🚓 Police Officer / EMS / Fire Dept — steady government paycheck + bonuses
- 🔧 Licensed Mechanic — full vehicle repair/tuning shop work
- ⚖️ Lawyer / Legal Advisor roles
- 🏗️ Construction Worker — bigger contracts, steady pay
- ✈️ Pilot / Delivery Pilot — cargo runs pay more than ground jobs
- 🚢 Boat/Cargo Runs — higher-value shipments

### 🏢 Mid-Level Office & Business Roles

- 💼 Business Manager (working for someone else's shop/company)
- 🏦 Bank Employee
- 📈 Real Estate Agent — helping players buy/sell property (commission-based)

### 🎮 Battlepass & Event Income

- 🏆 Weekly/Seasonal Battlepass rewards (cash + item rewards)
- 🎉 Server events, tournaments, or community challenges with cash prizes
- 📅 Daily/weekly quest streak bonuses

### 👥 Family & Gang (Legal Side) Bonuses

- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Business income — running a shared family-owned shop/store
- 🎖️ Leader Bonuses — a portion of gang/family profits shared by the leader to active members
- 🤝 Split earnings from group jobs (multiple players working one job together)

### 🎯 Mid-Tier Side Hustles

- 🛒 Running a small legal business/stall
- 🧵 Advanced crafting & selling higher-value items
- 🏠 Renting out owned property to other players

> 💡 **Tip:** This is where being active in a **gang or family** starts paying off — steady group activities + leader bonuses add up fast over a week.

---

## 🔴 TIER 3 — High-Risk / High-Reward Income (₹1 Million → ₹10 Million per week)

This tier is dominated by **illegal activity**, gang operations, and big business — high risk of arrest/loss, high reward.

### 🕵️ Illegal Criminal Activities

- 🏪 Store/Bank Robberies — high payout, high police attention
- 🚙 Car Theft & Chop Shop Sales — steal, strip, and sell vehicles
- 💊 Drug Production & Selling (white powder, green leaves — following server drug rules)
- 🔫 Illegal Weapon Trafficking
- 💰 Money Laundering through front businesses
- 🎰 Illegal Gambling / Casino Rigging Operations (if permitted by server rules)
- 🚚 Smuggling Runs (contraband, illegal goods across the map)
- 🏦 Major Vault/Heist Jobs (large coordinated crew heists)
- 🔓 Hacking / Cracking Jobs (ATM hacks, cyber-crime style RP)

### 🎭 Gang Activities

- 💵 Turf/Territory Control — controlling zones for passive income
- 📦 Gang-run Drug Labs or Warehouses
- 🤝 Protection Rackets (extorting businesses — if allowed by server rules)
- 🎖️ **Leader Bonuses** — gang leaders distributing large shares of gang profits to loyal/active members
- 🔫 Contract Hits / Mercenary Work (within RP rules, no RDM)

### 🏢 Big Business & Ownership

- 🏢 Owning a Business (your **1 allowed business** — Rule 1.5) — shop, dealership, nightclub, etc.
- 🏘️ Property Investment & Flipping — buying/selling houses for profit
- 📈 Running a large legal company with employees under you
- 🚗 Vehicle Dealership Ownership

### 🎮 High-Tier Events & Bonuses

- 🏆 Top-tier Battlepass completion rewards
- 🥇 Winning major server-wide tournaments/events
- 👑 Faction Leadership Bonuses (Gang Leader, Family Head, Business Owner payouts)

> ⚠️ **Risk Warning:** This tier carries real consequences — jail time, seized assets, and rival gang conflict. Always weigh risk vs reward, and never break rules like RMT (GR1.6) or tax evasion (GR1.9) trying to chase this money faster.

## 🧠 Beginner's Golden Rules for Money-Making

1. 🐣 **Start legal.** Build capital safely before risking illegal RP.
2. 👥 **Join a family or gang** — shared activities + leader bonuses multiply your income.
3. 🏢 **Save for one solid business** — remember, only 1 business per player (Rule 1.5).
4. ⚖️ **Balance risk.** Illegal money is fast but comes with jail, gear loss, and rival conflict.
5. 🚫 **Never break economy rules** chasing money — RMT and tax evasion get you **permanently banned**, wiping out everything you earned.

💬 Play smart, roleplay it out, and let your money grow naturally with your character's story! 😎
