# 📜 General Rules & Terms

Welcome! This guide explains the server's rules in **simple, beginner-friendly language**. Please read carefully — not knowing a rule does **not** protect you from punishment. 🙏

## 🧭 How to Read This Doc

Each rule has:

- 🔹 A simple explanation
- ⚠️ Examples of what NOT to do
- The punishment shown in a **☠️ Danger Zone** box

---

## 1️⃣ Account Rules

### 🔐 Rule 1.1 — Your Account = YOUR Property

🔹 You own your account. You **cannot** share, trade, borrow, sell, or give it away — this includes your game account, Epic, Steam, and Rockstar Social Club account.

⚠️ Examples:

- "Bro log in my account to play pls"
- "Selling my account cheap, DM me"

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN**

---

### 🔓 Rule 1.2 — Hacked Accounts Are Still Your Responsibility

🔹 Admins are **not responsible** if your account is compromised. Punishments stay even if you say you were hacked.

⚠️ Examples:

- "Someone hacked me, why am I banned?" → still banned
- "My friend used my account!" → still banned

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: NO REMOVAL OF PUNISHMENT — NO EXCEPTIONS**

**✔️ Stay Safe:**

- Don't share your account or login
- Turn on 2FA
- Avoid shady links/sites

---

### 🎭 Rule 1.3 — No Scamming Players

🔹 No scamming, tricking, or stealing money, cars, items, or property from other players.

⚠️ Examples:

- Fake trades or giveaways
- "Send money now, I'll pay later"
- Pretending to be staff
- "Trust trade" or gambling scams
- Paying less than the agreed amount on purpose

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN + NO COMPENSATION**

💡 If you get scammed, admins **will not** refund your loss — so trade smart.

---

### 👥 Rule 1.4 — Max 2 Accounts Allowed

🔹 You may only have:

- 1 Main Account
- 1 Extra Account (Twink)
- **Total: 2 accounts, tied to 1 Rockstar Social Club**

⚠️ Examples:

- Making a 3rd or 4th account (even on a different Social Club)
- Making extra characters to grind/abuse money
- Using an extra account to dodge punishment

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: ALL ACCOUNTS BANNED** (Main account may be banned up to **60 days**)

---

### 🏢 Rule 1.5 — Only 1 Business Per Player

🔹 One real person can own **only one** business on the server — no loopholes, no alt accounts, no hidden co-ownership.

⚠️ Examples:

- Business on Main + Business on Twink
- Secretly co-owning a business through a friend

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 30-DAY BAN ON ALL ACCOUNTS INVOLVED**

---

### 💵 Rule 1.6 — No Real Money Trading (RMT)

🔹 You cannot buy or sell **anything** in-game (cars, houses, items, accounts) using real money.

⚠️ Examples:

- "Selling car for ₹2000 / $20 PayPal"
- Paying real cash for in-game money, a job, or an advantage

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN — for BOTH buyer and seller, no warnings**

💡 Screenshots/proof of RMT = instant, permanent removal. Admins won't help if you get scammed doing this — it's against the rules to begin with.

---

### 💸 Rule 1.7 — Money Transfers & Gifting

🔹 You **can** gift or transfer money, but taxes apply above a certain limit.

**How it works:**

- ✔️ First $5,000,000 — no tax
- ✔️ Above $5,000,000 — **10% tax**
- ✔️ Family Balance Transfers — only **8% tax** (cheaper & safer)

⚠️ Examples:

- Sending $7M directly → 10% tax on the extra $2M
- Sending $20M via Family Balance → only 8% tax (better option)
- Splitting money in shady ways to hide it → suspicious, may be investigated

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Suspicious/abnormal transfers may trigger an investigation and punishment**

💡 Admins do **not** waive or remove tax.

---

### 🚫 Rule 1.8 — Twink Accounts Cannot Interact With Main

🔹 Your Main and Twink accounts are **strictly separate**. They cannot trade, gift, share, or help each other in **any** way.

⚠️ Examples (all forbidden):

- Twink sends money/items to Main (or vice versa)
- Hiding items in a shared Family car for the other account to collect
- Using a third player as a middleman to move stuff between your accounts
- Mailing/postal transfers between your own accounts
- Dropping items on the ground and switching accounts to pick them up
- Using Twink to farm resources for Main, or to dodge punishment

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN ON BOTH ACCOUNTS — logs track everything**

---

### 🧾 Rule 1.9 — Don't Cheat the Tax System

🔹 Follow the proper buying/selling system. Don't fake prices or hide transactions to dodge tax.

⚠️ Examples:

- Selling a $20,000,000 house for $1,000
- "Pay later" tricks to secretly pay the real price in cash afterward
- Trading items to avoid paying tax
- Using a middleman to pay privately
- Beach Market Stall trick: pricing one item super cheap and another wildly overpriced to hide the real price

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 60-DAY BAN — all accounts involved get punished**

## 2️⃣ Character & Nickname Rules

### 🧑 Rule 2.1 — Nickname Creation

🔹 Your in-game name must look like a **real, realistic human name**. No celebrity names, no meme names.

🔹 Nazism & Dictators in Names will be blacklisted.

⚠️ Not Allowed:

- KillerBoy_69, Noob Gamer, GTA Gamer Pro
- Michael Jackson, Elon Musk, Cristiano Ronaldo, or any admin's name
- Hitler, Stalin, etc names will be blacklisted instant.

✅ Good Examples:

- Jonny Bravo, John Pluxury, Jack Ripper or any other name that looks like a real person

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 10 Minutes Jail + Forced Nickname Change**

## 3️⃣ Communication & Chat Rules

### 🗣️ Rule 3.1 — No OOC Insults / Harassment

🔹 Don't insult, abuse, or harass anyone Out-Of-Character — in text, voice, PMs, or Discord.

⚠️ Examples:

- Calling someone "stupid," "trash," or "noob" OOC
- Harassing someone because you lost
- Toxic flaming or bullying

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 180-Minute Mute, up to 7-Day Ban depending on severity**

---

### 👪 Rule 3.2 — Never Mention Relatives / Family

🔹 Do not mention anyone's family members — not as jokes, insults, or "funny toxicity" — in any chat or voice channel.

⚠️ Examples:

- Insulting someone's mother, father, or siblings
- Dark jokes or threats involving family
- "Just joking, bro" does **not** excuse it

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 180-Minute Mute, up to 7-Day Ban**

---

### 🌐 Rule 3.3 — English-Only in Public RP Chat

🔹 Public roleplay chat must stay in **English** so everyone can follow along.
🔹 Other languages are only allowed in **private chat**, and only if everyone involved agrees.
🔹 Note: mild slang insults are tolerated _within limits_ inside RP chat only.

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 120-Minute Mute**
