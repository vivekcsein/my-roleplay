# 🎭 Roleplay Basics: IC, OOC, Metagaming, FailRP, FearRP, PowerGaming & UB Driving

Welcome, new roleplayer! 👋 This guide explains the **core roleplay concepts** every beginner must understand before playing. Not knowing these rules is **not an excuse** — read carefully. 📖

---

## 1️⃣ IC vs OOC vs Metagaming

### 🎮 What is IC (In Character)?

🔹 IC = everything your **character** does inside the game world — their talk, actions, decisions, and emotions. Not you, your character.

✔️ Examples:

- "Hands up, this is a robbery!" 🔫
- "Police, pull over!" 🚓
- "My character hates your gang" — a decision your character makes based only on what they know IC

---

### 💬 What is OOC (Out of Character)?

🔹 OOC = you talking as your **real self**, outside of roleplay — for clarifying issues, reporting, or tech problems.

✔️ Examples:

- "My game crashed"
- "Sorry, lag"
- "How do I use this command?"
- "Admin, I need help"

---

### 💀 What is Metagaming? (STRICTLY ILLEGAL)

🔹 Metagaming = using **OOC/outside information** in an **IC situation**. In simple words: your character acting on things they never actually learned in-game.

⚠️ Examples:

- Seeing someone's location on Discord → going to kill them in-game ❌
- Watching someone's stream to find their location ❌
- A friend PMs "police are coming" → you escape IC using that info ❌
- Identifying someone using their player ID or Discord name ❌
- "I know your name because I saw it above your head" ❌

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Warning → Mute/Jail → Ban depending on severity and repetition**

❗ **Simple Rule:** Outside knowledge stays outside. Your character only knows what happened _inside_ the RP world.

---

## 2️⃣ FailRP, FearRP & PowerGaming

### 💥 FailRP — Bad / Unrealistic Roleplay

🔹 FailRP = doing something that would **never realistically happen** in real life, or breaking RP logic on purpose.

⚠️ Examples:

- Jumping off a building "because respawn"
- Driving into the ocean for fun
- Purposely crashing into people to troll
- Ignoring an active RP situation happening around you
- **Combat logging** (leaving the game to escape RP)
- Using `/kill` to avoid a scenario
- Pretending you didn't see/hear obvious RP happening
- Refusing to roleplay when required

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Jail/Mute → escalating Ban for repeat offenders**

---

### 😱 FearRP — Value Your Character's Life

🔹 FearRP = you must act like your character genuinely **fears for their life** when in real danger — just like a real human would.

⚠️ Examples (NOT allowed):

- 4 armed people point guns at you — you punch and run ❌
- Gun to your head — you keep talking tough ❌
- Surrounded and kidnapped — you act fearless ❌
- Police aim rifles at you — you drive off like a movie hero ❌
- Someone clearly threatening your life — you joke about it ❌

✔️ Correct FearRP:

- Put your hands up when threatened
- Comply when multiple guns are aimed at you
- Value your character's safety over "winning"
- Don't act like an invincible action hero

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Jail/Mute → escalating Ban for repeat offenders**

---

### 💪 PowerGaming — Forcing RP or Impossible Actions

🔹 PowerGaming (PG) = doing unrealistic actions, or **forcing outcomes on other players** without giving them a fair chance to respond.

⚠️ Examples:

1. **Forcing actions without a chance to react**
   - "/me breaks your neck — you die instantly"
   - "/me handcuffs you instantly" with no reaction time
   - Running over a player who's already handcuffed

2. **Physically impossible actions**
   - Dodging bullets like a superhero
   - Fighting normally while multiple guns are pointed at you

3. **Medical/patching abuse**
   - Healing someone while holding a gun (put the gun away first!)
   - Patching a body that's still stuck under a car — remove the car first

4. **Vehicle reality breaks**
   - Magically exiting a locked car with no RP reason

5. **Identity/name unrealism**
   - Saying someone's name without proper IC identification (they must tell you, show ID, or you must have learned it IC — seeing a nametag/Discord name doesn't count)

6. **No Pain / God Mode RP**
   - Ignoring injuries, running full speed after being shot

7. **Unrealistic advantage-seeking**
   - "Surgery" mid-gunfight
   - Instantly changing clothes to dodge police recognition

8. **Hostage-taking rules**
   - One hand must stay free while taking a hostage
   - Only a revolver/pistol may be used to take a hostage — **no** assault rifles or shotguns

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Jail/Mute → 3–7 Day Ban → Permanent Ban for repeated/severe PowerGaming**

---

## 3️⃣ UB Driving & Unrealistic Behaviour (UB)

### 🚗 Rule 5.1 — Unrealistic (UB) Driving

🔹 UB Driving = driving in a way that no real human would, purely to gain an unfair advantage or break immersion.

⚠️ Examples:

- Ramming other cars/players for no RP reason (VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch)
- Driving at full speed through crowds, red lights, or police checkpoints with no RP reason
- Using a vehicle as a weapon to injure/kill instead of proper RP
- Impossible driving stunts (flips, wall-rides, no-damage crashes) used to escape RP
- Reckless driving purely to grief or troll other players
- Spawning/despawning vehicles mid-chase to escape realistically

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Jail/Mute → 3-Day Ban → escalating Ban for repeat offenders**

---

### 🎭 Rule 5.2 — General Unrealistic Behaviour (UB)

🔹 UB = any action that breaks basic realism or common sense expected in serious roleplay — beyond driving.

⚠️ Examples:

- Acting invincible after taking heavy damage
- Casually walking around after major injuries with no reaction
- Treating combat/crime like an arcade game instead of real RP
- Ignoring realistic consequences of your character's actions
- Random, senseless violence with no RP build-up (RDM — Random Deathmatch)

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Jail/Mute → escalating Ban depending on severity**

## ✅ Quick Summary Table

| Concept     | Simple Meaning                         | Danger Zone                       |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| IC          | Your character's actions/words in-game | N/A — this is normal RP           |
| OOC         | Real-talk outside of roleplay          | N/A — used for tech/reports       |
| Metagaming  | Using outside info in RP               | 🟠 Warning → Ban                  |
| FailRP      | Unrealistic/nonsensical actions        | 🟠 Jail/Mute → Ban                |
| FearRP      | Not valuing your life when threatened  | 🟠 Jail/Mute → Ban                |
| PowerGaming | Forcing actions / impossible RP        | 🟠 Jail/Mute → 7-Day → Permanent  |
| UB Driving  | Reckless/unrealistic vehicle use       | 🟠 Jail/Mute → 3-Day → Escalating |
| General UB  | Breaking realism/common sense          | 🟠 Jail/Mute → Escalating         |

---

## 💡 Beginner's Golden Rule

> **"Would this realistically happen to a normal human being?"**
> If the answer is **no** — don't do it. That's 90% of these rules in one sentence. 😎

Still unsure about a situation? Ask staff **before** acting instead of risking a punishment. Good roleplay = fun for everyone! 🎉
