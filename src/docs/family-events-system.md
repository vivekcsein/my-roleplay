# 🎪 Family Events System

## 📖 Overview

Events are an important part of family activity, teamwork, coordination, and progression.

There are **three types of events** available to the family:

1. 🔵 **OOC Events**
2. 🟠 **Unofficial IC Events**
3. 🟣 **Informal Events**

Each event type has its own communication and Roleplay requirements.

> ⚠️ **Always check the event type before participating. Different events follow different rules.**

---

# 🔵 OOC Events

## What are OOC Events?

**OOC (Out of Character) Events** are events where members are allowed to communicate with each other outside of the game.

### 💬 Communication

- You are allowed to stay in **Discord Chat** to communicate with other family members.
- Discord communication is allowed throughout the event.

### 🎭 Roleplay Rules

- You **do not have to follow IC rules** during OOC Events.
- However, **all General Rules still apply**.
- Breaking General Rules may still result in disciplinary action.

> 💡 **OOC does not mean that all rules are disabled. General Rules always apply.**

# 🟠 Unofficial IC Events

## What are Unofficial IC Events?

**Unofficial IC Events** are events where members must maintain proper **In Character (IC)** behavior.

These events are especially important for unofficial families.

### 💬 Communication Rules

- You **must communicate with other members through IC Chat or Family Radios**.
- Discord Chat should not be used as the primary method of communication during the event.

### 🎭 Roleplay Rules

- You **must follow IC rules**.
- Proper Roleplay is required.
- Members must remain in character throughout the event.

### 🚫 Leaving the Event

You are **not allowed to leave or escape from the event without a proper OOC reason**.

If you need to leave, there must be a valid OOC reason.

> ⚠️ **Unofficial IC Events = IC Communication + IC Rules + Proper Roleplay**

# ⚠️ General Event Rules

## 1. 🎯 Know the Event Type

Before joining an event, always check whether it is:

**🔵 OOC → 🟠 Unofficial IC → 🟣 Informal**

Each category has different rules.

---

## 2. 💬 Use the Correct Communication

### 🔵 OOC Events

**Discord Chat is allowed.**

### 🟠 Unofficial IC Events

**IC Chat communication is required.**

### 🟣 Informals

**Discord Chat is allowed.**

---

## 3. 🎭 Respect Roleplay

During Unofficial IC Events, members must:

- Stay in character
- Follow IC rules
- Communicate through IC Chat
- Follow the event scenario
- Avoid unnecessary OOC communication

---

## 4. 🚫 Do Not Leave Without a Reason

During Unofficial IC Events, members must not leave or escape from the event without a proper OOC reason.

---

## 5. 🤝 Follow Event Leadership

During family events:

- Follow the Event Leader
- Follow instructions
- Maintain proper communication
- Work together as a team
- Do not intentionally disrupt the event
- Respect other family members

---

## 6. 🛡️ General Rules Always Apply

Even during OOC Events where IC rules do not apply, **General Rules still apply**.

> **OOC ≠ No Rules**
