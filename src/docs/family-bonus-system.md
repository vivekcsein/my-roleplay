# 💰 Family Bonus System

## 🏆 How the Bonus System Works

Like most active families, we have our own bonus system to reward members who participate in events, get kills, survive battles, and help the family win.

The main idea is simple:

> 🏆 **Family Wins → Members Contribute → Members Get Rewarded**

Different events have different rewards, so make sure you check the event rules before joining.

---

# 🎟️ RP Factory Bonus

RP Factory is one of our most important events, so it has a special bonus system.

## 🏆 If the Family Wins & You Stay Alive

If you stay alive until the event ends and the family wins:

- 📝 **Registered Member** → 🎟️ **1 RP Ticket**
- 👤 **Unregistered Member** → 🎟️ **1 RP Ticket**

Your registration status does not change the RP Ticket reward for surviving.

---

## 💀 If You Die but the Family Wins

If you die during the fight but the family still wins:

### 📝 Registered Member

→ 💰 **$200,000**

### 👤 Unregistered Member

→ 💰 **$100,000**

So even if you don't survive until the end, you can still get rewarded as long as the family wins.

---

# ⭐ Event Leader & Event Manager Bonus

RP Factory also has an additional bonus for the people responsible for managing the event.

## 🛡️ Top 4 Event Managers

If the family wins, the **Top 4 Event Managers** receive:

→ 🎟️ **1 RP Ticket**
→ 💰 **$100,000 extra**

This bonus is given even if the Event Manager dies during the fight.

### 👑 Event Leader

The Event Leader gets:

→ 🎟️ **2 RP Tickets**

Only **1 Event Leader** can receive this bonus.

# ⚠️ Important Things to Remember

## 🏆 1. Most Bonuses Require a Win

For the bonuses mentioned above, the **family needs to win the event**.

No win = no applicable win-based bonus.

---

## 📝 2. Keep the Event Logged

For events where logging is required:

> ❌ **No Logs = No Rewards**

Make sure your kills, participation, and other required information are properly recorded.

### 🚨 Exception

**Family Raids** are an exception to the normal logging requirement.

---

## ⏳ 3. Claim Your Reward on Time

You have **48 hours** to claim your reward.

After 48 hours:

> 💰 **Unclaimed rewards go to Family Funds.**

So don't wait too long to claim your bonus.
