# 🛡️ Safety & Fair Play Rules — Doxing, Harassment, OOC Threats & Cheats

This guide covers the server's **zero-tolerance** rules around personal safety, respectful behavior, and fair play. These are some of the most serious rules on the server — please read every section carefully. 🙏

---

## 1️⃣ Doxing & Real-World Threats

### 🕵️ Rule 7.1 — No Doxing

🔹 Doxing means sharing, searching for, or threatening to reveal someone's **real-life personal information** — name, address, workplace, social media, phone number, photos, family details, etc. — without their consent.

⚠️ Examples:

- Posting someone's real name, address, or socials in chat/Discord
- "I know where you live"
- Digging up a player's real identity because of an in-game argument
- Threatening to expose someone's personal info if they don't comply

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN — no appeal**

💡 This applies **everywhere** — in-game, Discord, DMs, streams, or any other platform. It doesn't matter if it "was just to scare them."

---

### 🔪 Rule 7.2 — No Real-World Threats

🔹 Threatening real-world violence or harm against another player — even as a "joke" — is strictly forbidden.

⚠️ Examples:

- "I'll find you in real life"
- "Watch your back IRL"
- Threatening someone's real family, home, or safety
- Encouraging someone to self-harm

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN — reported to platform/Discord Trust & Safety where applicable**

---

## 2️⃣ Harassment

### 😡 Rule 7.3 — No Harassment or Targeted Bullying

🔹 Repeatedly targeting, insulting, or going after the same player or group — in-game or OOC — counts as harassment, **even if you frame it as roleplay**.

⚠️ Examples:

- Following one player around server after server to bother them
- Continuing to attack/insult someone after a conflict is already resolved
- Mocking someone for losing an RP scenario
- Coordinating with friends to gang up on one player repeatedly
- Harassing someone across Discord, DMs, or other platforms because of something that happened in-game

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Mute (180 min) → escalating to 7-Day Ban → Permanent Ban for repeat offenders**

💡 Staff look at **patterns across the whole community** — not just one clip. "I was just roleplaying" is not a valid excuse if the pattern shows targeted bullying.

---

### 🚫 Rule 7.4 — No Hate Speech or Discrimination

🔹 Zero tolerance for racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, or any other form of bigotry — **even if said "in character."**

🔹 This includes **any racial, ethnic, or ableist slur**, no matter how it's spelled, abbreviated, or disguised (e.g. typing it with symbols/numbers to dodge chat filters still counts).

⚠️ Examples:

- Using a racial or ethnic slur — IC or OOC, "as a joke" or not
- Using N and R words to insult someone's is strictly forbidden
- Using an ableist slur to insult someone's intelligence or disability
- Claiming "my character is racist" as an excuse to use slurs
- Discriminatory jokes in OOC or voice chat
- Mocking someone's identity, background, disability, or appearance
- Typing a slur with spacing, symbols, or alt spelling to get around auto-mod filters

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN — in-character framing does NOT excuse this, and filter-dodging is treated the same as saying it outright**

---

## 3️⃣ OOC Threats & Breaking Character to Intimidate

### ⚔️ Rule 7.5 — No OOC Threats

🔹 Using **Out-of-Character** chat, voice, or DMs to threaten, intimidate, or pressure another player is forbidden — whether it's about the game or real life.

⚠️ Examples:

- "Report me and see what happens"
- Threatening to get someone banned through false reports as revenge
- DMing someone to scare them into giving up items/money
- Breaking character mid-scene to threaten a player if they don't comply

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Mute (180 min) → up to Permanent Ban depending on severity**

---

### 🎭 Rule 7.6 — Don't Force IC Consequences Using OOC Pressure

🔹 You cannot threaten OOC action (like a bad report, real-life doxing, or harassment) to force someone's in-character decisions.

⚠️ Examples:

- "Comply in RP or I'll dox you"
- "Give me the car or I'll report you for something you didn't do"
- Threatening to leak private clips/screenshots to force compliance

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN**

---

## 4️⃣ Cheats, Exploits & Unauthorized Mods

### 🖥️ Rule 7.7 — No Cheats or Unauthorized Mods

🔹 Using any third-party software, injected client, or unauthorized modification to gain an advantage is strictly forbidden.

⚠️ Examples:

- Aimbots, wallhacks, ESP
- Speed hacks, teleport hacks, godmode
- Any anticheat bypass or injected client
- Stat/money manipulation tools

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: PERMANENT BAN — no appeal**

---

### 🐛 Rule 7.8 — No Exploiting Bugs or Glitches

🔹 If you find a bug or exploit, **report it** — don't use it for personal gain.

⚠️ Examples:

- Duplicating money, items, or vehicles
- Using a glitch to become invisible or invincible
- Exploiting a broken mechanic to make money faster
- Sharing exploit info with other players instead of reporting it

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: 30–60 Day Ban (or Permanent for repeat/severe abuse) + all illegal gains removed**

---

### 📄 Rule 7.9 — Anticheat Bans Require Proof to Appeal

🔹 If the Anticheat system bans you, you must provide a clip/proof to be considered for an unban.

⚠️ Examples:

- Getting flagged and claiming "it was a false positive" with no evidence
- Intentionally triggering the anticheat (e.g. shooting gas tanks) to test it, then asking to be unbanned

> ☠️ **DANGER ZONE: Ban stays permanent without valid proof**

## 📢 How to Report Instead of Retaliating

If you experience doxing, harassment, threats, or suspect someone is cheating:

1. 📸 **Save evidence** — screenshots, clips, timestamps
2. 🚫 **Do not** respond with threats or retaliation of your own
3. 🎫 Open a report ticket with staff (out of game, once any active scene is finished)
4. 🕒 Let the admin team investigate — they track logs and evidence carefully

💬 Your safety matters more than "winning" the argument. Report it, don't handle it yourself. 😊
